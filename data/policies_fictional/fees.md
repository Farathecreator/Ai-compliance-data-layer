# Account fees

## Monthly account fee
The Liffey Everyday current account has a monthly maintenance fee of €4.50. The fee is waived in any month where the account receives a deposit of at least €1,500.

## ATM withdrawals
ATM withdrawals in Ireland are free. ATM withdrawals in the euro zone outside Ireland are also free. ATM withdrawals outside the euro zone cost 2% of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum charge of €2.50.

## Unpaid item fee
If a direct debit or standing order is returned unpaid because of insufficient funds, an unpaid item fee of €10 is charged. No more than three unpaid item fees are charged in one month.
