# Transfers and payments

## SEPA transfers
SEPA transfers in euro to other banks are free. A standard SEPA transfer arrives within 1 working day. SEPA Instant transfers arrive within 10 seconds and are also free.

## Transfer limits
The daily limit for transfers to a new payee is €2,000 for the first 24 hours after the payee is added. After that, the daily transfer limit is €10,000.

## International transfers
Transfers outside the SEPA zone cost €15 per transfer and arrive within 2 to 4 working days.
