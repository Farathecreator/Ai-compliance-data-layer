# Cards

## Lost or stolen card
If your debit card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately in the Liffey app under Cards, then Freeze card. You can also call the 24-hour card line. A replacement card arrives within 5 working days and there is no charge for a replacement.

## Daily card limits
The daily contactless limit is €100 per transaction. The daily ATM withdrawal limit is €700. The daily online card spending limit is €5,000.

## PIN security
Liffey Bank will never ask for your full PIN, your online banking password or a one-time passcode by email, text message or phone call. Never share these with anyone.
