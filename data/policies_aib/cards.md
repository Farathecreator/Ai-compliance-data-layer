# AIB cards (source: aib.ie, How do I report my AIB card(s) as lost or stolen?, retrieved November 2026)

## Lost or stolen card
To report a lost or stolen AIB card in the AIB mobile app, tap Cards in the bottom tab, choose the card, tap the three dots for more, then tap Replace card and say whether the card is lost or stolen. In Internet Banking, choose the card and select Cancel and Replace my Card. Your new card is sent to your address.

## Card Services phone line
You can call Card Services on 1800 24 22 27, or on +353 1 771 5639 from outside Ireland, and select option 1 for Lost and Stolen. The line offers 24/7 support.

## Digital wallets
If the card is in a digital wallet, it updates automatically. Debit cards update by 5pm the next working day if you keep the same PIN. Credit cards update immediately.
