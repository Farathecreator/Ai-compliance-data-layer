# AIB personal account fees (source: aib.ie, A guide to fees and charges for personal accounts, 1 July 2026, AIBM/REG1 07/26)

## Monthly account fee
AIB charges €6.00 per month per account for personal current accounts where your account has been active that month. Accounts with no monthly fee include the AIB Basic Bank Account and the AIB Demand Deposit Account. A mortgage benefit applies where your mortgage payments are paid by direct debit from that account.

## Debit card ATM and purchase fees
AIB charges €0.12 each time you take out cash at an ATM. This is capped at a maximum charge of €2.50 per year for ATM use only, or €5.00 per year if you use your debit card for both ATM withdrawals and purchases.

## Non-euro card transactions
Non-euro card purchases cost 1.75% of the euro value, with a minimum of €0.45 and a maximum of €11.00. No minimum fee applies to contactless. Non-euro cash withdrawals cost 2.5% of the euro value. The cash withdrawal commission is 1% of the euro value, with a minimum of €2.00 and a maximum of €6.00.

## Services with no charge
Unpaid charges, and AIB Debit Card issue and replacement, are listed as services with no charge.

## Branch transfers
A domestic credit transfer made in a branch using a Paylink form costs €25.00 per item. For SEPA and international payment charges, see the current Schedule of International Charges booklet.
