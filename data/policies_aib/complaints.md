# AIB complaints (source: aib.ie, Make a Complaint, retrieved November 2026)

## How to make a complaint
You can make a complaint using the AIB online Complaint Form, by visiting, calling or writing to your local branch, or by writing to the Complaints Team, 10 Molesworth Street, Dublin 2, D02 R126. A member of the Customer Care team will contact you within five working days.

## If you are not satisfied
You can refer your complaint to the Financial Services and Pensions Ombudsman, which is an independent body. The Ombudsman expects you to raise the complaint with AIB first and will ask to see the final response letter.
